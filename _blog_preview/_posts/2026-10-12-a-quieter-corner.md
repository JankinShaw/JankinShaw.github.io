A personal website can be a remarkably small thing. A name, a few links, and some words that someone decided were worth keeping. It does not need to introduce everything at once.

There is something reassuring about arriving at a page that lets you read at your own pace. No panel asks for your attention. The text starts, and you can follow it as far as you like.

## The shape of a page

Typography gives that quietness a physical form. A line ends before your eyes lose their place. Paragraphs have room to breathe. A link looks like a link. These are small decisions, but together they make a page feel considered.

> A good reading page makes it easy to stay, and equally easy to leave.

The same care matters when languages share a page. 中文与 English 的混排，需要各自合适的字体，也需要自然的间距。A design that feels comfortable in one language still needs to be read and adjusted in another.

## A place to return to

Perhaps the most interesting part of a blog is how it changes over time. A short note becomes a question, and the question becomes something you revisit months later. The archive is a record of that movement.

The technology can stay simple. In this prototype, an article begins as a Markdown file:

```yaml
---
```

What fills the rest of the page can take its time.
