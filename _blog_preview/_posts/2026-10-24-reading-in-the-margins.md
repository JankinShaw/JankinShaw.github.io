A note in the margin can preserve something a highlighted passage cannot: what we were thinking when we read it.

## A way back

A question mark, a disagreement, or a connection to another chapter gives the next reading somewhere to begin.

> The margin is just large enough for an unfinished question.

Not every page needs a note. Sometimes a few words are enough.
