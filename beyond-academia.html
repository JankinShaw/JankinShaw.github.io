Beyond Academia

I enjoy British Parliamentary debate. My partner and I, competing as Epic Negotiators, made the top 24 at the 2022 national finals in China.

I like to share moments of the world as I see it.

I savour every moment on stage. At college, I once played Burr in Hamilton and the Fürst in a German-language version of Romeo and Juliet (Romeo und Julia).

I enjoy road cycling, especially uphill, and hiking, where I can daydream to the forest’s rustling symphony.
